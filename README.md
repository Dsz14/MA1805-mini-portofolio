01.Drawing - For the first portfolio, i have created an emoji with a live background. I have created a blood with shooting stars in the background where lines starts appearing at random locations that makes it identical to shooting stars. The background colour supports the theme as the primary colours for a blood moon are red, and orange. I also wanted to establish the agressiveness and the chaotic appearance of the blood moon by including colors that represent danger (red).

02.Movement - For the second portfolio, i wouldn't say it is my favourite canvas that I have made, but I wanted to illustrate fire represented on the bottom of the canvas and duplicated across the screen. I aso made the movement of the fire rapid considering that the movement of fire moves uncontrollably.

03.Multiple - My third portfolio was probably my favourite creation considering that it involves a variety of features relating to my 01.drawing and my 02.movement. This canvas is a mouse dragging interactive canva where you drag your mouse within the fitted ratio and bubbles will start to spawn and move in a unsteady motion. I made this creation to basically represent other peoples lives symbolising that bigger bubble represents a person who is living happily, and the smaller ones represent the opposite of happiness. I feel like this is my most creative yet 'real' digital art since it could reflect many people's lives.
