01.Drawing - For the first portfolio, i have created an emoji with a live background. I have created a blood with shooting stars in the background where lines starts appearing at random locations that makes it identical to shooting stars. The background colour supports the theme as the primary colours for a blood moon are red, and orange. I also wanted to establish the agressiveness and the chaotic appearance of the blood moon by including colors that represent danger (red). Whilst looking back at the live server, the drawing that I have created is quite overstimulating considering that there are a lot of things happening on the screen, which could also represent the sensory overload that is being established.

02.Movement - For the second portfolio, i wouldn't say it is my favourite canvas that I have made, but I wanted to illustrate fire represented on the bottom of the canvas and duplicated across the screen. I aso made the movement of the fire rapid considering that the movement of fire moves uncontrollably. It also has the ability where you clickon the screen, a message will come up, and if you restart the page, the colour of the fire and the text will turn into a more brighter yellow tint to it. 

03.Multiple - My third portfolio was probably my favourite creation considering that it involves a variety of features relating to my 01.drawing and my 02.movement. This canvas is a mouse dragging interactive canva where you drag your mouse within the fitted ratio and bubbles will start to spawn and move in a unsteady motion. I made the bubbles that have some transparency and slight visible colors inside of it, making it seem like it is animatedd from cartoons. I also created meaning from this creation to basically represent other peoples lives symbolising that bigger bubble represents a person who is living in true happiness, whilst the smaller ones represent people who are facing the opposite of happiness. I feel like this is my most creative yet 'real' digital piece of coding since it could reflect many people's lives.  

04.Media - For my fourth portfolio, I have created a interactive canva where the picture frame will pixelate and reduce the resolution when you hover the mouse in different directions of the screen. I have chosen the nature picture for this specific portfolio because it could give a nostalgic and retro aesthetic, it could evoke a sense of visual interest and style to it regarding to the deliberate, and blocky effect of the pixelation of the icture. it could also emphasise the unique, stylised approach that can make the picture stand out in a more minimalistic setting.
